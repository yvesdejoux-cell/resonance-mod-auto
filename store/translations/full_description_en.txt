ResonAuto is an open-source Android client for Navidrome and Subsonic-compatible music servers — listen to your own music library, on your phone and in your car.

What this fork adds to the original project:
🚗 Full Android Auto support — browse Artists, Albums, Playlists, Radios and Favorites right on your car screen, with "Play all" and "Shuffle" shortcuts, a heart button to add to favorites, and playback that resumes where you left off
🎶 Playlist management — create, rename and delete playlists, add or remove tracks from any screen, including the player
📻 Internet radio stations from your Navidrome server
🌍 7 languages: French, English, German, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese and Dutch, with a language picker in settings
🎧 Wide audio format support: formats your phone can't play (WMA, APE, WavPack, DSD, AIFF…) are converted by your Navidrome server
▶ Quick-play buttons on albums, artists and playlists throughout the app

Features inherited from the original project:
• Stream from any Subsonic or Navidrome server
• Browse by artist, album and genre, search your whole library
• Full playback controls: play, pause, skip, seek, shuffle, repeat
• Editable queue (play next, add, reorder, remove)
• Background playback with notification and media controls
• Mini player and full-screen player with gestures
• Favorites: artists, albums, songs
• Artist pages with biography and similar artists
• Synced and plain lyrics with auto-scroll
• Scrobbling to your server
• Several color themes
• No ads, no tracking — the only account used is yours

Requirements:
• A Subsonic-compatible server, for example Navidrome (navidrome.org)
• Android 12 or later
• To play non-native formats, a Navidrome server with ffmpeg (included in the official Docker image)
